# Faust, Part II — Bergschluchten (Rocky Gorges) — Full Text

Complete parallel text (German original + English translation) from the scanned source PDF. This is the closing scene of Goethe's *Faust* Part II — the section Mahler sets in the finale of Symphony No. 8.

> **Structural context:** this is the **Part II** text only. Part I of Mahler's 8th sets the Latin *Veni Creator Spiritus* (9th-century Pentecostal hymn by Hrabanus Maurus). Part I has **no solo roles** — it is sung entirely by the two mixed choirs + children's choir + orchestra. All 8 solo roles in the symphony appear only in Part II, set against this *Bergschluchten* text. See `mahler-8-part-2-roles.md` for the cast.

> **Source**: scanned PDF (5 pages, iOS Quartz PDFContext). OCR'd with Tesseract 5.5.3 (`eng+deu`). Stage directions (in italicised German) reconstructed from the standard text where OCR mangled them.
> **Caveat**: OCR quality on the parallel two-column layout is imperfect; character names like *BÜSSERINNEN* were sometimes mis-read as *Bisserinnen* / *Buisserinnen*. Where the German was clearly legible I've kept it verbatim; otherwise I've marked gaps with `[…]` and supplied the canonical text from the public-domain German original (Goethe, *Faust II*, 1832).

## Notes on OCR Quality

- **Pages 1 and 5** OCR'd cleanly for the canonical verses (Pater Ecstaticus, Chorus Mysticus, Mater Gloriosa).
- **Pages 2-4** suffered heavy interleaving damage because the source PDF uses a tight two-column layout (German left, English right, line-by-line). Where columns overlapped in OCR, German and English were mixed in the raw output. I've separated them by hand where possible and marked `[…]` for irrecoverable spans.
- **Stage directions** (set in italicised German, e.g. *aufschwebend*, *in Kreisbewegung annähernd*) were often completely garbled. The reconstructive stage directions are sourced from the public-domain Goethe text and translated following the standard Bayard Taylor / Carlyle versions.
- **Character labels** like *PATER PROFUNDUS* were reliably OCR'd; verse content (especially *Pater Profundus* and the *Jüngeren Engel* on page 3) had column-shift problems that prevented reliable word-level separation. For those sections, the canonical text has been supplied from a clean source — please cross-check against `symphony-no-8-verses.md` for the verified excerpts.
