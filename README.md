# Mahler — Symphony No. 8 in E-flat major

A research archive on **Gustav Mahler's Symphony No. 8 in E-flat major**, the work he sketched in roughly ten weeks during the summer of 1906 and premiered in Munich on 12 September 1910.

## A note on Part I vs Part II

Part I has **no solo roles** — the Latin hymn is sung entirely by the two mixed choirs, the children's choir, and the orchestra. Part II has **eight solo roles** that emerge only there. This is a deliberate structural and dramatic split: Part I is **divine / collective** (Creator Spirit, no individual), Part II is **human / individual** (Faust, Gretchen, individual souls). The Mater Gloriosa — the highest feminine figure, who bridges the two halves — is herself voiceless.
